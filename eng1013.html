Traffic Light Control System

A hardware-and-software traffic-light prototype that coordinates vehicle and pedestrian signal stages, using sensor inputs and a timed control sequence.

System overview

The project documentation describes a six-stage traffic-light sequence, incorporating main-road, side-road and pedestrian lights. Ultrasonic distance sensing and pedestrian push-button inputs were part of the proposed control system, alongside display and maintenance interfaces.

My contribution

  • Designed and documented the main traffic-light control sequence.
  • Developed the polling-timer design used to advance the sequence.
  • Designed the oncoming-vehicle distance-input routine and pedestrian button-count logic.

Engineering approach

The system was structured into control, input, service and output subsystems. The control design coordinates timed changes between signal stages, with input routines for ultrasonic vehicle-distance measurements and pedestrian requests. The video above shows the team's physical electronics prototype.
